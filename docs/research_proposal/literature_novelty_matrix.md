# Critical novelty matrix

**Evidence status:** primary publisher pages and the existing [Undermind novelty audit](https://app.undermind.ai/projects/abb3f852-eb61-47c9-a07a-481118bd6259?path=%2FPhase%202b%20-%20Adversarial%20novelty%20audit%20and%20three%20candidate%20gaps.md) were consulted. Undermind's search/PDF quota was exhausted on 11 November 2026 and reported a 17:00 UTC reset. Not every full text could be re-read in this session; the distinctions below must be rechecked before a paper claim. P = peer-reviewed; W = workshop/preprint.

| Work | What it solves / assumptions | Threat and distinguishing experiment |
|---|---|
| [Liu, Moon & Schorfheide, *Forecasting With Dynamic Panel Data Models*, Econometrica 2020](https://doi.org/10.3982/ECTA14952) P | Empirical-Bayes forecasting for many short individual panels; cross-sectional information and oracle-risk analysis under its dynamic-panel model. | Short-history shrinkage is established. Compare with a fitted EB dynamic panel, not an arbitrary fixed weight. |
| [Pesaran, Pick & Timmermann, *Forecasting with panel data: Estimation uncertainty versus parameter heterogeneity*, Quantitative Economics 2026](https://doi.org/10.3982/QE2589) P | Risk trade-off for pooled, individual and EB forecasts; optimal combination weights under stated panel assumptions. | Horizon-conditioned pooled/local weighting alone is incremental. Test heterogeneous reversion/shift beyond its fitted combination. |
| [Malenica et al., *Personalized Online Super Learner*, Statistics in Medicine 2023](https://doi.org/10.1002/sim.9655) P | Sequentially combines population and individual learners. | A learned global/local gate is occupied. Compare early-history and later-horizon harm against POSL-style weighting. |
| [Chen et al., *Learning to Extrapolate and Adjust*, IJCAI 2025](https://doi.org/10.24963/ijcai.2025/542) P | LEAF models macro-drift through latent-model extrapolation and micro-drift through online adjustment. | Stable/transient decomposition itself is occupied. Test new-entity persistence transfer at fixed horizons against LEAF where settings align. |
| [Ye et al., *Meta-iLaD*, ICML 2026](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ye26p.html) P | Meta-learns identifiable latent dynamics and environment variables from few-shot examples under locally stationary structural assumptions. | Few-shot latent-state identification is occupied. Any H2 theorem must state a different target or weaker assumptions. |
| [Huang & Michailidis, *PAC-Bayesian Meta-Learning for Few-Shot Identification of Linear Dynamical Systems*, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24117) R/TMLR status to verify | Transferable priors over task dynamics, dependence-aware predictive-risk analysis for linear systems. | A generic PAC-Bayes few-shot bound is occupied. Compare source mismatch and horizon-specific benefit rather than system-ID error alone. |
| [Janssens et al., *TMAML*, 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.04547) W | Temporally consistent support/query meta-tasks for cold-start forecasting; reported NeurIPS 2026 workshop acceptance. | Chronological few-shot meta-learning alone is occupied. Compare at equal history and backbone. |
| [Qin et al., *Deciding When to Use a Personalized Model for Load Forecasting*, IEEE TSG 2024](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSG.2024.3448618) P | Calculates fine-tuning gain and makes timestep-level global/personal choice in load forecasting. | Direct threat to any gain-prediction gate. Full method and test-split audit remain essential. |
| [Zhao et al., *Mixture of Online and Offline Experts*, AAAI 2025](https://doi.org/10.1609/aaai.v39i21.34448) P | Blends offline period experts and an online expert under nonstationarity, with regret/generalisation analysis. | Online mixing is established. Compare against correctly updated experts at equal information. |
| [Liang et al., *The Forecast After the Forecast*, ICLR 2026](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/6f7d90b1198fec96defd80b5ebd5bc81-Abstract-Conference.html) P | Bounded post-processing residual adapters and calibrated intervals. | Residual correction and bounded drift alone are occupied. |
| [Arnold et al., *Sequential model confidence sets*, JRSS B 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/jrsssb/qkag066) P | Anytime model comparison via e-processes, subject to conditional loss-difference conditions. | A confidence-triggered switch is incremental unless tied to a distinct persistence-learning result. |

## Verdict

The decomposition, horizon conditioning and generic gate each have **substantial overlap**. A claim could become distinct if a transferable estimate of *future evidence persistence* yields a new, identifiable benefit-risk target, an algorithm adapting to unknown persistence/source mismatch, and a clear held-out-entity gain over EB, Kalman and equal-capacity horizon gates. Presently this is an **unverified methodological hypothesis**. Full method/experiment audits of Qin, Pesaran, LEAF, Meta-iLaD and dynamic hierarchical state-space work are required before declaring novelty.
